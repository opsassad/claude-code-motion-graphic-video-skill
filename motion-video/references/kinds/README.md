# Kinds of video

The menu the person picks from at intake. Each kind has its own playbook in this folder: what it's for, what to ask, shapes it can take, the worlds and moves that suit it, and its pitfalls. A kind sets the job and the length; the concept (originality.md) still decides the story, and a style (styles.md) the look.

| Kind | One line for the person | Length | Playbook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch film | Reveal a product or a big feature, premium and memorable | 20–60 s | launch-film.md |
| Hero ad | One action filmed like a car ad; short, punchy, for paid social | 8–15 s | hero-ad.md |
| Explainer | Show how it works: a workflow, a flow, what happens behind the scenes | 30–90 s | explainer.md |
| Story film | A short film about a person's day, with the product inside it | 45–90 s | story-film.md |
| Character film | A mascot or character with a personality carries the film | 30–90 s | character-film.md |
| Kinetic type | Words are the visuals: an update, an announcement, a manifesto | 10–30 s | kinetic-type.md |
| Sizzle reel | Fast montage of many moments: energy and range | 8–20 s | sizzle.md |
| Teaser | A few seconds that make people curious before a launch | 5–10 s | teaser.md |
| Mood showcase | Atmosphere first: dreamy worlds, glass UI, emotion | 15–45 s | mood-showcase.md |
| Walkthrough | Teach a task step by step on the real screen (training, how-to) | 1–3 min | walkthrough.md |
| Showcase reel | A body of work: a portfolio, a year in review, a product line | 30–60 s | showcase-reel.md |
| Data story | One number or result made unforgettable | 10–40 s | data-story.md |

If the person isn't sure, ask what they want a viewer to *do or feel* afterwards, and suggest two kinds.
