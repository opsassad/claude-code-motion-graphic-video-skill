# Mood showcase

**What it is:** atmosphere first: dreamy worlds, frosted-glass UI, emotion. It sells a feeling (calm, ease, delight) as much as features.
**Pick it when:** consumer and AI products, brands that want to feel premium and effortless.
**Length:** 15–45 s.

## Ask the person
- What feeling should it leave (calm, wonder, confidence, joy)?
- Photographic worlds (nature, places), or soft gradients only?
- Any imagery they own or can license, or should images be generated?

## Shapes it can take
- **Worlds per beat:** each moment sits in its own world (sky, clouds, hills, a lake), and the UI floats in glass.
- **Exploded product:** UI fragments floating around a device with the camera orbiting.
- **The magic line:** one piece of content lifts out and turns into the product's output.

## Worlds and looks
Atmospheric glass (styles.md): mesh gradients or dreamy photos, frosted panels, glowing rims.

## Moves that often fit
`iris`, `pixelReveal`, `sweep`; `glowRim` on the hero object; `drift` for floating fragments; zoom-through into a photo; toast stacks for proof (`toasts`).

## Seen in the study
- A meeting-notes app: blossom sky → pink clouds → green hills → mountain lake; frosted prompt; a pixel-mosaic and an iris transition; one transcript line lifting out and becoming an action chip; an iris into a dark aurora for "Try it free."
- A real-estate marketing machine: a blush mesh gradient; payment notifications as the hook and the payoff; a listing photo growing into full-bleed; a focus carousel of vertical videos.

## Pitfalls
Pretty but empty: every beat still needs a product truth. Stock-looking imagery. Unreadable glass.
