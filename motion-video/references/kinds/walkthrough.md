# Walkthrough

**What it is:** teaches a task step by step on the real screen: training, onboarding, a how-to, a change notice.
**Pick it when:** staff or customers must *do* something correctly after watching.
**Length:** 1–3 min (30–90 s for a change notice).

## Ask the person
- The exact task and its steps, in order. Which step do people get wrong?
- Who watches, in what language, on what device, with or without sound?
- Rules to remember (shown as callouts)?

## Shapes it can take
- **Old way → new way:** the old way briefly, crossed out, then the new way once, then one rule.
- **Step by step:** each step = the camera to the control, the click, the result, a caption card.
- **Mistake first:** show the common mistake and its consequence, then the right way.

## Worlds and looks
The real screen at true size, the camera moving within it; caption cards in the viewer's language. Walkthrough (styles.md).

## Moves that often fit
`cursor` + `clickZoom`; camera poses on the window; `morphRect`; caption cards with `rise`; `countUp` for totals.

## Seen in our own work
Training videos for support agents, in their own language, shared on a messaging app: the real internal tool rebuilt, the camera zooming to each control, the cursor clicking, numbered step cards, and "remember" callouts for rules.

## Pitfalls
Too fast. Text too small on phones. Steps without the why.
