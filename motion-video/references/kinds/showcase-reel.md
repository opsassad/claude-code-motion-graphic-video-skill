# Showcase reel

**What it is:** a body of work in one film: a portfolio, a product line, a year in review, an agency reel.
**Pick it when:** the point is breadth and quality across several pieces.
**Length:** 30–60 s.

## Ask the person
- Which pieces, in what order of importance? For each, the one-line story and one detail only it has.
- Is it about a person (portfolio) or a company?
- Links, logos, credits to show.

## Shapes it can take
- **Before → after of a career or company,** then the work, then the mark.
- **Each piece as a card with its own signature moment** beside it.
- **Everything at once:** the whole body of work in one frame, then one piece at a time.

## Worlds and looks
The person's or company's own site look. Card showcase (styles.md).

## Moves that often fit
3D fly-ins on a spring, whip-outs with `motionBlur`, `rise` for titles, `wall` for everything at once.

## Seen in our own work
A portfolio reel: the real site cards flying in, each project's own story animated beside it (a ringing phone opening the caller's record, a coupon toast, an AI filter typing itself), whip-outs, the animated logo ending.

## Pitfalls
A flat carousel. Equal weight for every piece. A camera roving through one big "world".
