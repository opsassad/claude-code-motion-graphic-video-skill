# Kinetic type

**What it is:** words are the visuals. Short, rhythmic, cut to the music. For updates, announcements, statements, event openers.
**Pick it when:** the message matters more than a demo, there's little UI to show, or it's a monthly "what's new".
**Length:** 10–30 s.

## Ask the person
- The exact words (or the points to turn into words). What's the one line people must remember?
- Music: tempo or a track? (this kind is cut to the beat)
- Brand fonts and colours.

## Shapes it can take
- **The metaphor acted on the type:** the product's idea performed by the words (for a collaboration tool, multiplayer cursors drag the headline into place; for a writing tool, the words edit themselves).
- **Beat-cut statements:** one word or phrase per beat, scale jumps between huge and small.
- **One word becomes the message:** the key word appears, grows into the sentence, then the logo.

## Worlds and looks
Two or three colours, high contrast; a design grid or registration marks; occasionally text as a window onto footage.

## Moves that often fit
`assemble`, `wordToSentence`, `maskWords` + `rise`, `wordTexture`, `typeText` with an accent swap, word → icon, letters dropping out, the echo stack, a logo-shaped portal transition, `onBeat` for timing.

## Seen in the study
A monthly update: app icons raining around the lockup; hard cuts to huge words on dark; the brand's AI name as a window onto 3D footage; named collaborator cursors rotating and scaling "Workspace Drops" into place; a typed word turning into the sparkle icon, which then grows into a portal.

## Pitfalls
Too many words per frame. Motion on every word, so nothing stands out. Ignoring the beat.
