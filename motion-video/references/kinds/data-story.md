# Data story

**What it is:** one number or one result made unforgettable: impact, growth, a benchmark, a finding.
**Pick it when:** the proof is the message (results posts, investor updates, reports).
**Length:** 10–40 s.

## Ask the person
- The number(s), their source, and whether they're public.
- What does the number mean for a real person? (the human translation)
- The comparison that makes it vivid (before/after, vs. competitors, vs. something everyday).

## Shapes it can take
- **One number, built up:** context → the number lands as it's said → what it means.
- **Before vs after:** two states side by side, the difference highlighted.
- **The journey:** a flow or funnel, stage by stage.

## Worlds and looks
Clean light with one accent, or dark with a single bright data colour. Real charts, simplified.

## Moves that often fit
`countUp`, `cascade` for bars or rows, a flowing ribbon (funnel), `growToWord` for the headline number, `drawPath` for lines.

## Pitfalls
Several numbers fighting. Animating before the viewer knows what the number means. Unsourced or unapproved numbers.
