# Teaser

**What it is:** a few seconds that make people curious, before a launch or as a loop.
**Pick it when:** "coming soon", a waitlist, a story or Reel, a website hero loop.
**Length:** 5–10 s.

## Ask the person
- What can be shown, and what must stay hidden?
- The date or the call to action ("join the waitlist", "Oct 30").

## Shapes it can take
- **One striking image, one line.**
- **The zoom-out reveal:** one tile → many → the platform (it's bigger than you thought).
- **A glimpse:** a sliver of the product through a shape, a crop, a blur.

## Worlds and looks
A vivid brand gradient or a single bold colour; one mark.

## Moves that often fit
`wall` with `emerge` (zoom-out reveal), `iris`, `typeText`, `develop`, a sandwich ending (headline above, line below).

## Seen in the study
An 8-second platform teaser on an iridescent gradient: one blank card types its title, pulls back into a grid of agents, the grid becomes the app's home screen, sandwiched between "The Intelligence Age is here." and "And we've built the foundational infrastructure."

## Pitfalls
Explaining too much. Showing everything. No date or action.
