# Sizzle reel

**What it is:** a fast montage of many moments (0.25–0.5 s each) that sells energy and range.
**Pick it when:** event openers, a category intro ("everything AI can do"), a year in review, a platform with many features.
**Length:** 8–20 s.

## Ask the person
- Which moments or products to include (10–25)?
- The music (essential: it's cut on the beat).
- The title it should land on.

## Shapes it can take
- **Accelerate then land:** slower shots speeding up to a rush, then one title.
- **A rhythm of states:** generating, analysing, creating, sending... each a different visual.
- **Many products, one idea:** each shot a different product doing the same kind of thing.

## Worlds and looks
Mixed media is welcome: UI, macro type, material type, real device footage. A shared colour grade keeps it one piece.

## Moves that often fit
Hard cuts with `onBeat`; whip smears (`motionBlur`); `shimmer` and `thinking` states; macro crops of huge UI text; push into a screen until it's only light, then the title.

## Seen in the study
An AI sizzle: ~20 shots in 10 s: glass 3D cards generating output, a model list expanding, debossed paper type with letters flying off, a red sphere rolling over serif text, a hand holding a glowing phone, the camera pushing into the screen, the title resolving.

## Pitfalls
Shots with no single idea. Cutting off the beat. No landing.
