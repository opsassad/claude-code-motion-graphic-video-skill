# Taste: lessons from real feedback

These lessons come from real client rounds: versions that were rejected and versions that were loved. They apply to almost everyone. A person's own taste file (`MOTION_TASTE.md` in the project, or `~/.claude/motion-video-taste.md`) overrides them.

## What got rejected, and why
- **An invented look.** Dark motion-template styling around a brand whose site is light and clean. It reads as "not us", however slick it is. *Fix:* the video looks like the product's own website.
- **Busy screens.** Particles, grain, glow specks, dark caption boxes and big extra titles all at once. *Fix:* one card plus one animated moment per scene, and plenty of space.
- **Flat motion.** A plain carousel of screenshots sliding sideways felt like a slideshow. *Fix:* depth (3D fly-ins), weight (springs), speed (whips with motion blur).
- **A gimmick holding the whole video together.** One continuous "connected world" with a roving camera tried too hard, and the message got lost. *Fix:* hand-offs used once or twice, not as the backbone.
- **Vague hooks.** "I was the user." Clever, but it says nothing. *Fix:* concrete before-and-after with a year, a role and a number.
- **Washed-out colour.** A texture or halftone effect that greyed the artwork. *Fix:* keep the original colours, and make texture a darker shade of each colour.
- **Extra asks at the end.** "Let's talk", "Book a call" added without being asked. *Fix:* end on the mark and one link.
- **Mispronounced names.** It ruined an otherwise good take. *Fix:* phonetic spelling in the style block and in the script.
- **A launch video made of separate scenes.** Each screen was right, but they just followed each other, and it read as a slideshow, not a motion piece. *Fix:* connect the screens with shared-element morphs along the product's real flow (see craft.md).
- **Forcing in a look from a reference.** Small tilted cards dropped into three moments, to copy a reference's angled screens, looked bolted on. *Fix:* when a reference shows a look, describe what you see in it (camera, framing, focus, movement) and confirm it before building.
- **The wrong logo.** The project had a `logo.svg` that wasn't the brand mark. *Fix:* look at the logo candidates and confirm which one the live site uses.
- **UI too small to read.** A whole app window in a square feed video is unreadable on a phone. *Fix:* camera zooms on the part in use.
- **Every film the same.** A blind test gave four runs the same brief (an AI form builder): all four made "type a request → the form builds → edit by chat → toasts → montage → slogan → logo", and the runs with this skill copied its demos' order even more closely. The person's verdict: "the AI is the same shit". *Fix:* originality.md: human truth first, 8 angles, kill tests, banned backbones, demos for mechanics only.
- **Moves used because they exist.** A typing close-up on a shop's search box: fine, but it wasn't the shop's real action (choosing and buying), and it's the start of every video looking the same. *Fix:* moves only where the subject has that action; build 1–2 moments unique to the subject.
- **A wrong image under a label.** "Batteries" showed a solar panel because the file was never opened. *Fix:* look at every image before using it.
- **A price counting up from 0.** It looked like the price was changing. *Fix:* count only totals and growing amounts.
- **Descriptions instead of pictures.** ASCII layouts and long text proposals cost rounds with non-technical people. *Fix:* render stills and show them.

## What got loved, and why
- **Real cards from the real site** flying in, with the project's own story animated beside them (a ringing phone opening the caller's record, a coupon toast, an AI filter typing itself). It was recognisably theirs and alive.
- **Specific, true numbers landing as they're spoken.**
- **A rhyming open and close:** the hook echoed in the final line.
- **An animated logo ending** with the site link drawing under it. Quiet, confident.
- **The voice fitted exactly,** each scene starting with its line.

## Keeping a personal taste file
After each round of feedback, add short entries:
```markdown
## Loves
- <what> — <why, in their words if possible> (<date>, <video>)
## Rejects
- <what> — <why> (<date>, <video>)
## Fixed preferences
- Captions: <none / srt / burned-in>. Voice: <provider, voice name>. Endings: <...>. Logos: <...>.
```
Read it before every concept. If a lesson would help anyone, add it here too, without names or private details.
