# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and constructive, especially in reviews: critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect other people's time: keep issues and pull requests focused.
- Give credit where it's due, including to the creators of reference work you studied.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information, or anyone's work without permission.
- Spam, or repeated off-topic or bad-faith comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break this code. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer, [@opsassad](https://github.com/opsassad), on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
